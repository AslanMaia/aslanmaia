## Research

**Stochastic Modeling and Optimization of the Operation of Hybrid Energy Communities with Storage**  
Undergraduate research (Iniciação Científica) funded by FAPESP · Advisor: Prof. Dr. Marcos Julio Rider Flores · Apr 2026 – present

- Two-stage stochastic MILP for the sizing and hourly operation of a residential PV + battery system under uncertainty in solar generation, demand and grid outages.
- Implemented in Python with Pyomo and Gurobi.
- Next step: hydrogen storage (electrolyzer, tank, fuel cell).

Code: [`stochastic-energy-opt`](https://github.com/AslanMaia/stochastic-energy-opt)
